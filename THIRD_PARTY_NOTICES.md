# Sources, licences and original work

LATENT is an independently authored portfolio for Harsh Bhatt. No landing page, paid template, Pro component, stock portrait or Envato asset is copied.

## Owner material

Biography, project facts and reported metrics are audited against `CV.md`, extracted from the owner's `Harsh-Bhatt-CV (3).pdf`, plus the explicit owner brief. `CONTENT_AUDIT.md` records corrections. The authentic portrait and vCard came from the read-only original site. The downloadable CV is the new owner PDF with its phone text redacted; it is not a byte-identical original. No telephone field ships in the site or vCard. The original site's animated stats were verified in Chrome: 2 manuscripts, 5 projects, ~100K prompts and 9 months' industry experience.

Six prelabelled dataset examples are exact public first-rows API rows 0, 1, 2, 3, 13 and 18 from [Generalized Hallucination Risk Level](https://huggingface.co/datasets/hb0810/Generalized_Hallucination_Risk_Level_100k_Prompts). Dataset wording and labels are not generated or inferred by this website. The score is labelled confidence. Attribution and dataset terms remain with its Hugging Face card.

The preserved AVIF was converted, without cropping or retouching, to a 906x805 WebP (quality 92) for consistent texture decoding, including Windows WebKit builds that lack AVIF. Original and derived-image provenance accompany the assets. `public/ASSET_ORIGINS.json` records their SHA-256 hashes. The OG image is an actual rendered application screenshot, not generated imagery.

## ThreeUI Community

Meng To's [ThreeUI Community repository](https://github.com/MengTo/threeui), snapshot `68802d5428071ada5c20db8094b1649e6bb770ed`, was cloned into gitignored `_reference/threeui`. The actual Community `SemanticBloom`, `TypographyVortex` and `LiquidMetalButton` implementations were studied for particle organization, restrained continuous motion and metallic interaction vocabulary. The site's GPU morph, geometry, camera rail, controls and shaders are original implementations. No iframe or extra component canvas is embedded. Requested examples without an actual Community implementation were not used. The upstream MIT copyright and permission notice are preserved in `third-party/ThreeUI-MIT.txt`.

## Design tools

Impeccable 4.7 and Taste Skill v2 were installed as local development skills. Their source folders and reference clone are excluded from Git. The current Taste package provides `design-taste-frontend`; no separate `output-skill` exists in that package. The user's explicit completeness requirements are followed. The exact owner-pinned LATENT concept takes precedence over randomized alternative layouts and house defaults that prohibit the explicitly requested loader, mono telemetry or chapter indices.

21st.dev Magic was not connected and no API key was available at implementation time. The owner explicitly permits the handmade fallback. All console, navigation, controls, disclosures, composer and feedback are authored for this design. No MCP retrieval is claimed.

Envato logo-reveal references inform motion vocabulary only: particle assembly, liquid settling and an industrial stamp. No Envato media or project file is included.

## Fonts

Archivo Black and IBM Plex Mono are self-hosted Fontsource packages under the SIL Open Font License. Copies are in `third-party/Archivo-Black-OFL.txt` and `third-party/IBM-Plex-Mono-OFL.txt`. The 4.5KB decorative 3D name typeface is an Archivo Black glyph subset converted from the Google Fonts TTF using Three's TTFLoader/OpenType parser. It is not a default Three.js typeface. The actual accessible name and all portfolio copy remain DOM text.

## Offline portrait processing

The authentic owner photograph is locally matted with [rembg](https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg) and its `u2net_human_seg` model. rembg is MIT licensed; its Python/ONNX toolchain and downloaded model weights remain local build tools and are not shipped. Sharp (Apache-2.0) handles offline pixel access. The mask and cutout preserve the original face and clothing; no generative retouching or uploaded photo service is used. The custom bake performs alpha/edge/luminance importance sampling, a vignette and spatial pairing with the exact deterministic HB surface positions. Public binaries are derived owner assets, not a third-party portrait dataset. Origins and SHA-256 values are recorded in `public/ASSET_ORIGINS.json`.
